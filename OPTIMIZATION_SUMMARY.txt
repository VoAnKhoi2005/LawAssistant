2. FIND_COMMON_ANCESTORS PERFORMANCE
   ✓ Optimized with bulk fetching (lines 342-429)
     - OLD: N * max_depth * 2 queries (per section traversal)
     - NEW: max_depth * 2 queries total (batch traversal)
     - Example: 100 sections, depth=3
       * OLD: 100 * 3 * 2 = 600 queries
       * NEW: 3 * 2 = 6 queries
     - ~100x speedup for large result sets!
